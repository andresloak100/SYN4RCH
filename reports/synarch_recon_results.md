The writer of this benchmark read that report before writing the protocol. Nothing here is confirmatory.

'every / some / none' counts the comparators that the engine beats. 'Any amount' compares means over brain samples and is satisfied by a difference of any size, also one far below what could matter. The interval rule also asks the descriptive 95% bootstrap interval of the paired difference to exclude zero. The minimum gain is 2% of the comparator's own value (declared before the run). A task is `exploratory_supported` only if the engine beats EVERY comparator with the interval rule and the minimum gain; otherwise the API returns the baseline-level answer, flagged, or refuses. **The verdict of a task is that last comparison**: the engine beat every, some or none of the comparators clearly (interval and minimum gain). The two 'any amount' columns are shown because they are the literal comparison of means; a model that learned nothing satisfies them about half of the time.

Multiplicity: 4 tasks, one primary metric each. The status rule above is the only decision taken from this benchmark. Every other interval in this report (other metrics, single comparators, source labels) is an unadjusted description and decides nothing.

## Limits

- Exploratory: the regional table was on disk and had been modelled before this benchmark was written. No result here is confirmatory, and no status is 'validated'.
- Regional lengths are 49 published summaries on the scale log10(1 + length); they are not a partition of the brain, their unit is indicated but not resolved, and MY contains IO, NTS, PARN, IRN.
- Intervals describe the fitted distribution, not parameter uncertainty.
- A back-transformed log mean is not an expected length; only quantiles back-transform exactly.
- Nothing here is a 3D path, a synapse, gene expression, or a causal effect of a label.
- No validation or test brain sample was read; no trace was opened.
